# Better Command History - v1.0.0
## Initial Release
### PLUGIN UPDATES
**Prefix history search**: Type the start of a command, then press Up to cycle only through past commands that begin with it. The more you type, the narrower it gets.
**No repeats**: A command you ran several times shows up once, at its most recent position.
**Down to return**: Down steps back to newer matches, then restores what you typed.
**Saved history**: Commands are remembered across restarts, and each one is forgotten after 7 days without use.
**Mod Menu settings**: Turn saving off, change how long commands are kept (1 day to never) and how many, or clear your history.
**Version support**: Available for Minecraft 1.21.11, 26.1, 26.2 and 26.3.
